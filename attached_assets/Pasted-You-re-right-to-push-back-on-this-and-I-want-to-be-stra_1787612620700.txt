## Where the complexity actually lives

| Piece | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|

## The honest bottom line
Two things drove the bloat: (1) the review loop kept asking "is this production-hardened for healthcare?" and we kept saying yes, and (2) the notification feature came in enterprise-grade. **But almost all of it is invisible to your users and already paid for.** Tearing it out now is *new* work and *new* risk for zero user benefit.

So my recommendation, even in "map" mode: **simplify the roadmap, not the codebase.** Concretely —
- **Keep** the core workflow, migrations, tests, and consent capture.
- **Leave** the DB hardening and notification engine as-is (built, tested, invisible). Mentally file them under "already done, don't extend."
- **Stop** adding infrastructure. The next steps to launch are *not* more code — they're the DB repair + smoke test, and eventually the telehealth vendor call.

The one place a real trim would buy you a simpler *mental model* (not a simpler product) is the notification subsystem — I could replace it with a plain "send email + SMS on new request, log on failure" if carrying the outbox engine bothers you. But I'd only do that if it's actively nagging you; otherwise it's working and it's done.
